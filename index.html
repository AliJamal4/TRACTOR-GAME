Wheat & brick season · Punjab, 2010

Kacha Rasta

A city on the GT Road, four villages on broken mud roads, a brick kiln and a sand quarry. Haul bags, bricks, sand and loose wheat. Every kilo changes how your tractor pulls, grips and steers.

  • 01Talk to people. Haji Bashir at the mandi pays freight for wheat. Thekedar Aslam at the city site needs sand and bricks.
  • 02Tipper trolleys dump their load with hydraulics. At the quarry, dig sand yourself with the excavator or ask the operator.
  • 03Rain, punctures, police nakas, buffalo crossings and price swings happen on the way. Spawn any of 7 tractors and 7 trolleys at the workshop.
