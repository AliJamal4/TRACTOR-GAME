Galla Mandi season · Punjab, 2010

Kacha Rasta

Haul wheat in gunny bags from the farms to the town grain market. Every bag changes how your tractor pulls, grips and steers.

  • 01Walk to your tractor, climb on, and follow the orange beacon to the farm. Labourers load the trolley while you wait.
  • 02Bags stacked ahead of the trolley axle press on the hitch and lift the tractor’s nose. The canal bridge is the real test.
  • 03Earn rupees at the mandi, then buy a bigger tractor, a 4x4, or a dual-tyre tandem trolley at the workshop.
